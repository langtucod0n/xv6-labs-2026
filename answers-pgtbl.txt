1. Loi giai su dung bao nhieu PTE superpage?
- Su dung 95 PTE superpage (32 PTE cho vung PLIC va 63 PTE cho vung RAM tu 0x80200000 den PHYSTOP 0x88000000).

2. Nhung PTE superpage nay tiet kiem duoc bao nhieu trang o cap cuoi?
- Tiet kiem duoc 95 trang bang trang o cap cuoi (L0 page-table pages), tuong duong 95 * 4096 = 389,120 bytes (khoang 380 KB).
- Ly do: Moi superpage 2 MiB thay the cho 512 trang 4 KiB o cap cuoi. Thay vi phai cap phat mot trang bang trang cap 0 chua 512 muc PTE cap 0, superpage dat truc tiep leaf PTE o cap 1 (L1), loai bo hoan toan nhu cau cap phat trang bang trang cap 0 do. Do do, moi superpage tiet kiem chinh xac 1 trang bang trang cap cuoi.
